#+TITLE: HTTP
#+OPTIONS: ^:{}

The routes under =/_/= that the page calls (=src/lib/api.ts=). Everything sent is encrypted first; see [[file:../architecture/encryption.org][Encryption and access]].

* Conventions

+ =<root>= is a root's name, percent-encoded. A name that isn't a single valid segment: =400=.
+ Bytes in JSON are base64url without padding.
+ Errors are JSON, ={"status": 401, "message": "/x is locked"}=.
+ Every route but =GET=s needs =Sec-Fetch-Site: same-origin=, or an =Origin= matching the page's when the browser doesn't send that. Otherwise: =403=.
+ Routes marked /unlocked/ need the unlock cookie for =<root>=. Otherwise, or if the root is unclaimed: =401=.
+ Routes marked /metered/ draw on a bucket per root; those marked /open/, on one per address (IPv6 per /64). Each request costs its body's bytes, at least 16 KiB. Out of it: =429= with =Retry-After= in seconds.
+ Routes marked /quota/ add to the root's bytes and pads and the address's new bytes for the day. Past the root's: =413=; past the address's: =429=; the server past its ceiling: =507=. A write that adds nothing always passes.
+ The numbers, and why: [[file:../architecture/capacity.org][Capacity and limits]], and =GET /_/limits=.
+ Behind Caddy the client's address is the last =X-Forwarded-For= hop (=ADDRESS_HEADER=, =XFF_DEPTH= in =deploy/pad.service=). Bodies are capped at 1 MiB (=BODY_SIZE_LIMIT=).

* Auth

=src/routes/_/[root]/auth/[action]/+server.ts=. All =POST=. Claim, unlock and lock are open; password and delete, metered.

** POST /_/<root>/auth/claim

Sets a free root's password and unlocks it for this browser. A deleted root counts as free: it's purged first.

Body: ={"salt": 16 bytes, "auth": 32 bytes, "sealedKey": 60 bytes}=.

+ =204=, with the unlock cookie.
+ =409= if the root was claimed meanwhile.

** POST /_/<root>/auth/unlock

Body: ={"auth": 32 bytes}=.

+ =200= ={"sealedKey": 60 bytes}=, with the unlock cookie. A deleted root is reclaimed.
+ =401= for a wrong =auth=. After 5, each failure doubles the wait, up to 15 minutes; meanwhile =429=, with the wait in the message. A browser whose device cookie has unlocked the root before is counted on its own; any other per address, IPv6 per /64. Nothing is counted per root, so no one can keep others out of it.

A successful claim, unlock or password change also sets the device cookie, =__Host-pad-device=, and records it for the root.

** POST /_/<root>/auth/password

/Unlocked./ Body: ={"current": 32 bytes, "salt": 16 bytes, "auth": 32 bytes, "sealedKey": 60 bytes}=: the current =auth=, then the new password's. =current= is checked and backed off as in unlock.

+ =204=. Every other browser's unlock of the root is revoked.

** POST /_/<root>/auth/delete

/Unlocked./ Marks the root and its pads deleted, and signs every browser out of it. =204=.

Until it's purged, 30 days on, unlock with its password reclaims it; claim purges it and claims it anew.

** POST /_/<root>/auth/lock

Ends this browser's unlock of the root. =204=, unlocked or not.

* Pads

** GET /_/<root>/pads

/Unlocked./ Every pad of the root.

=200= ={"pads": [{"id": "…", "name": "<sealed path>"}]}=. A root has at most 5000 pads.

** GET /_/<root>/pads/<id>

/Unlocked./ =<id>= is 43 base64url characters.

+ =200=, =application/octet-stream=: the sealed text, with =X-Updated-At= in Unix milliseconds.
+ =204= if there's no such pad, or it's been emptied.

** PUT /_/<root>/pads/<id>

/Unlocked, metered, quota./ Saves a pad; the last write wins. Saving an emptied pad brings it back.

+ Body: the sealed text, at most 512 KiB + 28 bytes; over: =413=.
+ =X-Pad-Name=: the sealed path, base64url, at most 1400 characters.

=200= ={"updatedAt": <ms>}=.

** DELETE /_/<root>/pads/<id>

/Unlocked, metered./ What saving an empty pad does: marks it, so it leaves the list and its links 404, and purges it 30 days on unless it's saved again. =204=.

* Theme

=src/routes/_/[root]/theme/+server.ts=.

** GET /_/<root>/theme

/Unlocked./ =200=, =application/octet-stream=: the theme, ={"bg": "#rrggbb", "fg": …, "ink": …}= sealed under the root's content key. =204= if none was ever picked.

** PUT /_/<root>/theme

/Unlocked, metered./ Body: the sealed theme, at most 256 bytes; over: =413=. =204=.

* Read-only links

=src/routes/_/[root]/shares/[share]/+server.ts=, =src/routes/_/[root]/pads/[id]/shares/+server.ts=. =<link>= is 22 base64url characters, 16 bytes the browser picks.

** GET /_/<root>/pads/<id>/shares

/Unlocked./ The pad's links, oldest first.

=200= ={"shares": [{"id": "<link>", "sealedKey": 60 bytes, "createdAt": <ms>}]}=.

** PUT /_/<root>/shares/<link>

/Unlocked, metered, quota./ Makes a link.

+ Body: the copy, deflated and sealed under the link's key, at most 1 MiB; over: =413=.
+ =X-Pad-Id=: the pad's id. =X-Share-Key=: the link's key sealed under the root's, 60 bytes.

=200= ={"createdAt": <ms>}=. =409= if the root has 1000 links, or the id is taken.

** GET /_/<root>/shares/<link>

/Open./ Anyone with the link may. =200=, =application/octet-stream=: the sealed copy, with =X-Created-At= in Unix milliseconds. =404= if it was revoked or never made, or its pad is emptied or its root deleted.

** DELETE /_/<root>/pads/<id>/shares

/Unlocked, metered./ Revokes every link to the pad. =204=.

** DELETE /_/<root>/shares/<link>

/Unlocked, metered./ Revokes the link. =204=, whether it existed or not.

* Limits

** GET /_/limits

Every limit in effect. Sizes in bytes, times in milliseconds; =writes=, =open= and =growth= are buckets of ={"burst", "perSecond", "floor"}=.

=200= ={"padBytes": 524316, "rootBytes": 16777216, "rootPads": 5000, "serverBytes": …, "writes": {…}, …}=.

** GET /_/<root>/usage

/Unlocked./ What the root's quota counts: its pads' sealed text and paths, emptied ones included until purged, and its links.

=200= ={"bytes": <n>, "pads": <n>}=.
