#+TITLE: Using pad
#+OPTIONS: ^:{}

* Paths

Any path is a pad: =/notes=, =/notes/today=, =/notes/today/later=. Pads are made by typing in them and deleted by emptying them: an emptied pad leaves its parent's list, and its read-only links stop working; typing in it again within 30 days brings the links back, and after that it's gone for good. A path has up to 10 segments of up to 64 characters, 255 in all; =/_/…= is reserved for the API.

* Roots and passwords

The first segment is the path's root: =/notes/today= belongs to =notes=. In the address bar, what's under the root comes after a =#=: =pad.nassu.online/notes#today/later=. Browsers never send that part, so the server knows which root you're in but not which pad.

+ A root nobody has saved under is free. Opening it asks for a password (at least 8 characters), which then locks the root and every pad under it, for reading as well as writing.
+ A locked root asks for its password. Five wrong ones are free; after that each waits twice as long as the last, up to 15 minutes. A browser that has unlocked the root before, even if it locked since, only waits for its own wrong passwords; any other only for its address's (for IPv6, its /64's). No one else's wrong passwords ever make you wait.
+ *password*, in a pad's header, changes the root's password. Every other browser is signed out of the root.
+ *lock* signs this browser out of the root and forgets its keys.
+ *delete*, in a root's header, asks first, then deletes the root and every pad under it, stops its read-only links and signs every browser out. For 30 days the root's page offers *reclaim it*: its old password brings back all of it, links included. Claiming the root anew instead, or 30 days passing, deletes it for good.

An unlock lasts 30 days unused, at most 180. The password can't be recovered: the pads are encrypted with it, so losing it loses them.

* Saving

A pad saves itself 600ms after you stop typing, when it loses focus, and when the tab is hidden. The status in the header says =unsaved=, =saving…= or =saved=; on a failure it retries every 5 seconds. Two people in one pad: the last save wins. A pad with nothing unsaved picks up others' saves every 5 seconds.

A pad holds 512 KiB, and a root 16 MiB and 5000 pads, its read-only links included; emptied pads count until they're gone for good, 30 days on. Each address adds at most 1 GiB a day. Every limit is in [[file:../architecture/capacity.org][Capacity and limits]].

* Read-only links

*share*, in a pad's header, lists the pad's read-only links. *new link* makes one and copies it: a copy of the pad and every pad under it as they are right then, which anyone with the link can read but not change. Later edits don't reach it; make a new link to share them. *revoke* deletes the copy, and the link stops working; *revoke all*, pressed twice, does it for every link to the pad.

The link's page shows the pad, and under it the pads under it, each opening within the copy; *edit* goes to the real pad, for those with the password. A link holds 1 MiB once compressed, which the dialog says when a pad and its subpads don't fit. A root holds 1000 links at most, and they count toward its 16 MiB.

* Themes

*theme*, in a root pad's header, picks the root's colours: pad's own, made for long reading (dusk, the default, and dawn), or one of [[https://github.com/NasreddinHodja/konigslibrary][konigslibrary]]'s (one bark, bubblegum, paper, white), or a background, text and ink of its own. Every pad under the root takes it, and so do read-only links made after. It's sealed like the pads, so the password page shows it only in a browser that has unlocked the root, until that browser locks it.

* Export

*export*, in a pad's header, saves the pad and every pad under it as a zip of org files: exporting =/notes= gives =notes.org=, =notes/today.org=, =notes/today/later.org=. Pads that only have pads under them are just folders. The zip is made in the browser, so the server still never sees the text. An export takes everything a root can hold.

* Finder

The field under the pad finds and makes pads under it.

| key                          | does                                           |
|------------------------------+------------------------------------------------|
| =ctrl+/=                     | opens it from anywhere; inside it, closes it   |
| =↑= / =ctrl+k= / =ctrl+p=    | the next match up                              |
| =↓= / =ctrl+j= / =ctrl+n=    | the next match down                            |
| =↵=                          | opens the match, or makes the typed path if none |
| =ctrl+↵=                     | makes the typed path, even when something matches |
| =esc=                        | closes it                                      |

Matching is fzf-like: the typed characters in order, word starts and runs ranked first.
